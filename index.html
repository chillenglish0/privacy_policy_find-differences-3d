Collected information is used to operate the game, provide leaderboards, serve ads, measure ad performance, improve the service, fix bugs, and support users.

Information is deleted when no longer needed or when deletion is requested, unless retention is required by law.

4. Third Parties

Information may be shared with the following partners for ads, analytics and cloud services:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, AdMob)

Each partner processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information where applicable. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

6. Ads and Cookies

The game uses advertising identifiers to provide ads. You can change your ad consent choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy settings in the game, and you can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings. Ads may still appear, but they will not be personalized.

7. Children

This game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when laws, policies, or security practices change. Updates are posted on this page.
